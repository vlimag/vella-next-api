# Vella Creator Pilot — Brazil

Use this brief for the Month 1 creator validation wave. Adapt the greeting and
reason for contacting each person; do not mass-send identical messages.

## Initial outreach

> Oi, [nome]. Eu acompanhei seu conteúdo sobre [referência específica] e gostei
> especialmente da forma como você fala de fé de um jeito prático e honesto.
>
> Estou lançando a Vella, um app cristão com Bíblia, oração e reflexão diária. Um
> dos recursos principais é um Espaço de Oração privado: a pessoa registra o que
> está vivendo, encontra uma passagem bíblica de uma edição aprovada e pode
> lembrar depois das orações e dos momentos de gratidão.
>
> Estamos procurando poucas pessoas para testar a experiência de verdade e, se
> fizer sentido, criar um vídeo curto mostrando como ela caberia na rotina. Não
> queremos um depoimento decorado nem uma promessa religiosa; queremos sua
> opinião e sua voz reais.
>
> Posso enviar um acesso e uma proposta simples com o escopo e o valor?

## Creator test instructions

Give the creator access before asking for content. Ask them to use Vella on at
least three different days and test:

- onboarding and subscription disclosure;
- Daily Light and Scripture attribution;
- Prayer Space, using only test text they are comfortable discarding;
- notification timing and language;
- cancellation/management path.

Collect private feedback before commissioning the final asset. Do not require a
positive review.

## Deliverables

- One 15–30 second vertical video in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Two Story frames or equivalent short posts.
- Vella and the real interface visible during the first three seconds.
- Captions burned in or supplied as a transcript.
- One raw, watermark-free export for Vella-owned organic reuse and, only when
  separately agreed, paid amplification.
- Clear disclosure such as `Publicidade`, `Parceria paga`, or the platform's paid
  partnership label when compensation or another material benefit applies.
- One unique campaign link supplied by Vella.

## Creative prompt

Choose one honest angle:

1. “Quando faltam palavras para orar.”
2. “Um lugar privado para lembrar suas orações.”
3. “Uma mensagem de fé no horário que você escolhe.”
4. “Uma prática curta de Bíblia, oração e reflexão.”

Suggested structure:

1. A real-life moment or question — 2–4 seconds.
2. The relevant Vella screen in use — 8–15 seconds.
3. One specific benefit experienced — 4–8 seconds.
4. Transparent offer and Android CTA — 3–5 seconds.

## Required truth boundaries

Creators must not claim that:

- Vella predicts or guarantees answered prayer;
- using Vella cures anxiety, depression, insomnia, or another condition;
- AI writes or translates the Bible inside Vella;
- Vella replaces a church, pastor, therapist, doctor, emergency service, or
  trusted human support;
- the entire app is free.

When mentioning the offer, use:

> 14 dias grátis no plano anual para novos assinantes elegíveis. Depois,
> R$X/ano, com renovação automática. Cancele antes do fim do teste para não ser
> cobrado.

Do not show a real prayer, private note, email, notification token, purchase
screen with personal details, or another person's content.

## Compensation and rights checklist

Agree in writing on:

- fixed fee and payment date;
- deliverables and revision limit;
- organic usage term;
- whether paid amplification is included, its term, platforms, and territory;
- whether raw footage is included;
- disclosure requirement;
- permission to show the creator's name/handle;
- campaign link and reporting window;
- right to decline publication if the experience does not feel authentic.

At the R$1,000 monthly learning budget, prefer several small authentic pilots to
one expensive endorsement. Do not promise affiliate revenue until attribution
and payout rules are reliable.

## Evaluation scorecard

Evaluate after the link has had enough time to mature:

- unique store visitors;
- installers;
- verified trial starts;
- first meaningful sessions;
- D7 meaningful use;
- trial-to-paid conversion after day 14;
- cancellations/refunds;
- usable creative quality and audience feedback.

Do not rank creators by clicks alone. A smaller creator who produces retained
paid subscribers and reusable footage is more valuable than a larger creator
who produces cheap, low-intent installs.
