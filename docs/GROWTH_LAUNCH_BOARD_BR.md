# Vella Brazil Android — 30-day launch board

Last updated: 2026-08-06

This board turns the R$1,000 Month 1 budget into one controlled learning cycle.
Do not start the paid phase until the corresponding readiness gate is green.

## Non-negotiable gates

| Gate | Evidence required | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Android listing | Public Play page opens in Brazil and the website sends Android visitors to it | Product | Ready |
| Annual offer | One real eligible account sees 14 free days and the correct local annual renewal price | Product | Verify before spend |
| Checkout | Purchase, server validation, restore, cancellation, expiry, and webhook state are correct | Product | Verify before spend |
| Measurement | First open through verified subscription and first value appear in the operator report | Product | Live; complete one real Android funnel test |
| Privacy | No prayer, search, preference, profile, token, receipt, or belief data appears in growth events | Privacy | Live and regression-tested |
| Creative truth | Every asset shows real UI and uses the approved trial disclosure when pricing is mentioned | Growth | Review before publish |

If checkout, entitlement, privacy, or content safety fails, pause acquisition. A
cheap install is not useful when a user cannot safely become a retained
subscriber.

attribute in an identifier.

## Four launch creatives

### A. When words are difficult

- First-frame copy: `Quando faltam palavras para orar…`
- 0–3s: Vella Prayer Space home, no personal content visible.
- 3–9s: enter neutral demonstration text such as `Uma decisão importante`.
- 9–15s: show approved Scripture and the private prayer step.
- 15–22s: mark the demonstration item as remembered or answered.
- End card: `Um espaço privado para oração, Bíblia e gratidão. Vella para Android.`
- Primary metric: verified annual trials per 100 installs.

### B. A private prayer memory

- First-frame copy: `Você lembra pelo que orou há três meses?`
- 0–5s: show the private timeline with fabricated demonstration entries.
- 5–12s: show a check-in and gratitude state.
- 12–19s: show the privacy boundary in the product.
- End card: `Lembre, ore e reconheça o que mudou.`
- Required caption: `Textos demonstrativos. Suas orações não são posts públicos.`
- Primary metric: first Prayer Space completion and D7 meaningful use.

### C. A message at the chosen time

- First-frame copy: `Um lembrete de fé no horário que cabe na sua rotina.`
- 0–4s: show notification-time selection.
- 4–10s: show an actual Portuguese notification containing approved Scripture or
  a complete encouraging sentence—not only “open the app.”
- 10–17s: open the corresponding Daily Light screen.
- End card: `Você escolhe o horário. Você pode desligar quando quiser.`
- Primary metric: notification opt-in and D7 meaningful use.

### D. A calm short rhythm

- First-frame copy: `Nem todo dia precisa começar correndo.`
- 0–4s: quiet opening on Daily Light.
- 4–11s: read one attributed verse.
- 11–17s: complete one reflection/prayer step.
- 17–22s: show completion without a guilt-based streak or exaggerated promise.
- End card: `Bíblia, oração e reflexão em poucos minutos.`
- Primary metric: first meaningful session and three-day weekly activation.

## Asset checklist

For each winning concept produce:

- 1080 × 1920 vertical video, 15–30 seconds;
- 1080 × 1080 square cut;
- 1200 × 628 landscape cut;
- one square static image with no more than one promise;
- burned-in Brazilian Portuguese captions;
- clean opening frame readable on a small screen;
- real current Android UI;
- no private account or devotional data;
- source file, final export, transcript, creative ID, and usage-rights record.

Do not place the subscription disclosure in unreadably small type. If the
creative mentions the offer, use the actual local annual price shown by Google:

> 14 dias grátis no plano anual para novos assinantes elegíveis. Depois,
> R$[PREÇO]/ano, com renovação automática. Cancele antes do fim do teste para
> não ser cobrado.

If the price has not been verified, do not mention the trial in that asset.

## Day-by-day execution

### Days 1–3 — prove the path

- Complete all readiness gates.
- Record the four raw product walkthroughs.
- Publish Creative A organically from Vella.
- Send the Android website link through a small trusted test group using the
  `vella_site` or `whatsapp` source convention.
- Confirm landing CTA, install, onboarding, paywall, checkout, validation, and
  first-value events in order.

Spend ceiling: R$0.

### Days 4–7 — establish an owned baseline

- Publish Creatives C and D on separate days.
- Publish one Portuguese Prayer Space article excerpt and one product demo.
- Log questions, objections, broken steps, and misleading expectations.
- Do not optimize from clicks alone; inspect qualified activation.

Spend ceiling: R$0.

### Days 8–10 — creator onboarding

- Contact eight carefully selected micro-creators using the creator pilot brief.
- Target three to five real product tests, not eight forced endorsements.
- Give access and ask for three days of use before commissioning a post.
- Issue a distinct normalized campaign and creative ID for each participant.

Committed creator ceiling: R$400 total.

### Days 11–17 — creator message validation

- Publish no more than one or two creator assets per day so results remain
  interpretable.
- Capture raw-footage rights and disclosure evidence.
- Compare qualified trials, first value, and early repeat use—not follower count.
- Select two messages for the paid Google test.

Spend ceiling to date: R$400.

### Days 18–30 — one Google learning campaign

- Create one Brazil/Portuguese Android App Campaign.
- Load the two winning vertical videos plus square and landscape adaptations.
- Use approximately R$46/day for 13 days, capped at R$600.
- Start with Maximize Conversions and do not invent a target CPI or CPA.
- Avoid repeated bid, budget, geography, or asset changes during learning.
- Record daily spend in the operator growth report.
- Review mechanical failures daily; review marketing performance weekly.

Month ceiling: R$1,000.

## Decision calendar

- Day 7: Is the funnel mechanically trustworthy?
- Day 17: Which two promises produced the most qualified users?
- Day 30: Is there enough signal to continue the channel? Do not yet declare the
  14-day cohort profitable if it is immature.
- Day 38–51: Review matured creator and Google trial conversion.
- Day 61+: Review D30 retained-paid outcomes for the earliest cohorts.

## Scale, hold, or stop

Scale by at most 20%–30% in the next budget period only when two mature cohorts
are below the provisional allowable CAC and guardrails remain healthy.

Hold when the signal is promising but sample size or cohort age is insufficient.

Stop and repair when any of these occurs:

- systemic checkout or entitlement error;
- ad/store promise does not match the product;
- trial starts but first value is consistently weak;
- cancellation, refund, or support complaints materially increase;
- the winning metric is cheap installs with poor devotional use;
- any sensitive user content enters analytics or advertising systems.
